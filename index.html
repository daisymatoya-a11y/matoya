What to do if you have lost an item

Follow the steps below in order. Do not visit the office before submitting a report — a reference number speeds up collection.

  1. Search the register

    Check the found items register to see whether your item has already been handed in.

  2. Submit a lost item report

    Complete the report form. Give a full description — a distinctive mark or serial number helps verify ownership.

  3. Wait for confirmation

    If a matching item is handed in, the office will email you at your student address with a reference number.

  4. Collect in person

    Bring your student ID to Room 12 during opening hours. Items will not be released without proof of identity.
